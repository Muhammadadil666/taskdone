Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email support@kanect.info. A real person reads it.

How do I share a task with someone?

Go to More → Friends & Family → Invite and send them the link over WhatsApp, Messages, anywhere. When they open it you're connected both ways, and either of you can give the other a task or do one together.

If they'd rather type a code, they can install the app and enter it under More → Friends & Family → Join.

Doing a task together

When you add a task, choose Do it together under "Who's it for?" and pick the people. It shows on everyone's list, and when one of you ticks it, it's done for all of you.

My invite code stopped working

Codes work once on purpose, so a link forwarded into a group chat can't connect everyone who sees it. After someone uses your code the app makes a new one: open Invite again and share the fresh link. Codes also expire after 14 days.

Do I need an account?

Only to share. Kindo works on its own without signing in. The account is what lets two phones agree on who is connected to whom.

I missed a few days

Nothing happens. There are no streaks in Kindo, so there's nothing to lose. Tap any earlier day at the top of Tasks and tick things off then.

How do I change, skip or delete a task?

Press and hold the task. You can Edit it, Skip today (it comes back tomorrow and doesn't count as missed) or Delete it.

I logged the wrong amount

Open the task (water, steps, pages, sleep…) and tap Edit next to the total to set the exact number, or clear it.

I'm not getting reminders

  • Check More → Notifications is switched on.
  • Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Kindo is allowed.
  • If you set quiet hours, reminders inside that window are held back on purpose.

Prayer times look a few minutes off

Times are worked out on your phone using a calculation method. Kindo picks the usual one for your country; to change it, or to switch Asr to Hanafi, press and hold your Prayers task, tap Edit, then Change how times are worked out.

What is a poke?

A nudge to someone about a specific task you gave them, so they know exactly what you mean. You can poke twice a day per task, at least four hours apart.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: More → Account → Delete account. That permanently removes your profile, tasks, ticks, connections and invites from the server. Or email us and we'll do it for you.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, nothing locked. The tip jar under Support is optional and unlocks nothing.
